# Grave Wounds {.cover}

## The king's baggage: one fight and the march to Calais

Version {{pt_version}}, for 3 to 5 people and about 3 hours. One person is the GM.

**What this is.** Grave Wounds is a d100 game built on real wound records. Where a man is hit comes from
the graves and surgeons' returns of his own war. A fight usually ends with men stopping, not dying, and
what happens afterwards can kill them just as well. This module sets four Englishmen with the king's
baggage at Maisoncelles on 25 October 1415, while the battle of Agincourt is fought a mile to the north.
They are not in the line. The raiders who fall on the baggage are their fight, and then the army's
march to Calais, with the wounded.

**What we want to learn.** Are the fights tense and quick enough? Does a fight over plunder play
differently from a fight to the finish? Do wounds feel fair? Nobody has played this at a table yet, so a
blunt "this dragged" is the most useful thing you can tell us.

**What you need.** Pencils, two ten-sided dice for d100, scissors, and these pages. Everything to play on
paper is here, with a hex map of the baggage and counters to cut out at the back. If you'd rather, a phone or
laptop can run the fight: **the wound roller does the arithmetic**. Open [{{pt_link_short}}]({{pt_link}}). It
sets up the four men against the raiders, and the GM's script can play the raiders. Please tell us which way you played.
