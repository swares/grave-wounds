    3 Blood a round; pain step P2; a stop check, Nerve {{pt_foe_nerve}}, less {{pt_foe_pain}} for pain; and, left
    untreated, it kills in hours. Donald's musket is now empty: he shoots again in the {{pt_musket_every}} round.
