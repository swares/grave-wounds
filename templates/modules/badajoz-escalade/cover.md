# Grave Wounds {.cover}

## The escalade: one fight on the rampart of Badajoz, and the week after

Version {{pt_version}}, for 3 to 5 people and about 3 hours. One person is the GM.

**What this is.** Grave Wounds is a d100 game built on real wound records. Where a man is hit comes from
the graves and surgeons' returns of his own war. A fight usually ends with men stopping, not dying, and
what happens afterwards can kill them just as well. This module sets four men of a British company of foot
at the foot of a ladder against the bastion of San Vicente at Badajoz, on the night of 6 April 1812, while
the storm of the breaches fails on the far side of the town. Their fight is to get up the ladder and hold
the rampart. Then the town is sacked, and the week after it is theirs to get through.

**What we want to learn.** Are the fights tense and quick enough? Do the ladders and the reloading make the
fight different from a medieval one? Do wounds feel fair? Nobody has played this at a table yet, so a blunt
"this dragged" is the most useful thing you can tell us.

**What you need.** Pencils, two ten-sided dice for d100, scissors, and these pages. Everything to play on
paper is here, with a hex map of the rampart and counters to cut out at the back. If you'd rather, a phone or
laptop can run the fight: **the wound roller does the arithmetic**. Open [{{pt_link_short}}]({{pt_link}}). It
sets up the four men against the French on this map, with the ladders, the night and the fires, and the GM's
script can play the French. Please tell us which way you played.
