# A worked example: the section {#example}

The Rules follow one medieval retinue. This chapter follows a section of B Troop, 3-61
Cavalry, at [Combat Outpost Keating](#keating-2009) on 3 October 2009, from the first RPGs at
dawn to the helicopters after dark. It uses the same rules and the modules this book switches
on, and, where dice are rolled, the dice given. The [team builder](team.html) made the section.

!!! example "The section"
    The team builder rolls {{mx.team}} of the US Army. **{{mx.lead}}** leads it:
    {{mx.lead_terms}} terms, so he is {{mx.lead_age}}, with Command {{mx.lead_cmd}}. Its
    machine gunner is **{{mx.mg}}**, who fires the gun at **{{mx.mg_att}}%**. Its grenadier
    is **{{mx.gren}}**, and its medic is **{{mx.med}}**, Leechcraft {{mx.med_skill}}.
    {{mx.followers}} riflemen follow them. Their kit: {{mx.kit}}.

## On the field map {#example-field}

!!! example "The section: the bowl"
    The outpost lies at the bottom of a bowl, and the insurgents hold the slopes on three
    sides. Men among the rocks show little of themselves: only {{mx.x_seen}}% of the
    section's aimed shots find a man to aim at, and {{mx.x_mg_seen}}% of its machine gun's,
    as in [Modern infantry](rules.html#modern-infantry). The rest go at the flashes.

    Then the Apaches come (the accounts differ on when). A gunship is called like a battery and fires
    {{mx.a_gs_rate}} rounds an exchange, {{mx.a_gs_rounds}} in all, as in
    [Aircraft](rules.html#aircraft). After each run it can be hit by the guns it attacked:
    {{mx.a_mg}}% for each machine gun served near the aim, doubled (×{{mx.a_exposed}}) because
    a gunship flies low and slow. With no air controller the aircraft will not fire within
    {{mx.a_danger}} of their own men. The insurgents are inside the wire, so the troop's
    leaders have to talk them in.

## In the roller {#example-roller}

The heroes' wounds are rolled in full, as in [the fight](rules.html#fight).

!!! example "The section: the plate"
    An insurgent PKM gunner, a regular, fires at {{mx.mg_first}} at {{mx.p_attack}}% and rolls
    **{{mx.p_roll}}**: a margin of {{mx.p_margin}}. Against a bullet, the body takes a margin
    of {{mx.p_band}} or more to be serious, so this one is **{{mx.p_sev}}**.
    {{mx.mg_first}} dodges at {{mx.p_def}}% and rolls {{mx.p_droll}}: no.

    The round strikes his **{{mx.p_loc}}**. His plate covers {{mx.plate_cover}} tens of the
    chest, and the units digit of the attack roll is {{mx.p_digit}}, so the round meets the
    {{mx.p_armour}}. The plate takes {{mx.p_steps_text}} off a rifle round, and the round is
    **{{mx.p_after}}**: he has a bruise and a hole in his vest. Had the roll ended in 7 or
    more, the round would have found the {{mx.p_soft}} beside the plate, which takes
    {{mx.p_soft_steps}} off a rifle round, and the wound would have stayed
    **{{mx.p_soft_after}}**.

!!! example "The section: the RPG"
    An RPG bursts by the truck where {{mx.gren_first}} is firing. Its thrower fires at
    {{mx.r_attack}}% and rolls **{{mx.r_roll}}**: a margin of {{mx.r_margin}}. A leg takes a
    margin of {{mx.r_band}} or more to be serious, so this is **{{mx.r_sev}}**.
    {{mx.gren_first}} dodges at {{mx.r_def}}% and rolls {{mx.r_droll}}: no.

    A fragment goes into his **{{mx.r_loc}}**, below his vest. {{mx.r_fracture}}It bleeds
    {{mx.r_bleed}}, pain {{mx.r_pain}}, and the leg is hurt. The stop check: Nerve
    {{mx.r_nerve}}, less {{mx.r_pain}} steps of pain, gives **{{mx.r_target}}**. He rolls
    {{mx.r_sroll}} and {{mx.r_result}}. At {{mx.r_per}} Blood a round, his {{mx.r_blood}}
    Blood lasts {{mx.r_rounds}} rounds.

!!! example "The section: the tourniquet"
    {{mx.med_first}} has the best Leechcraft in the section, and the GM rules that he can
    reach the truck in time. On a leg he goes straight to a tourniquet, at Leechcraft
    {{mx.t_skill}}, and rolls {{mx.t_roll}}: it holds, and the bleeding stops, as in
    [First aid](rules.html#first-aid). Then he drags {{mx.gren_first}} into cover.

    No helicopter can land while the fight goes on. When the battle ends with the troop
    holding a landing zone and a helicopter left, the wounded go out by air, as in
    [After the battle](rules.html#after), and {{mx.ev_heli}}% of those whose wounds would kill
    them in hours are saved. Then {{mx.gren_first}}'s wound goes onto the track, as in
    [Recovery](rules.html#recovery).

!!! gm "For the GM"
    In these wars armour and medicine change what a hit means. Many rounds that would have
    killed a man in 1916 now leave him with a bruise, and a man bleeding from a leg lives if
    someone gets a tourniquet on him in time. Keep the roller for those moments, and let the
    players read their own plates' cover on the attack roll's last digit.
