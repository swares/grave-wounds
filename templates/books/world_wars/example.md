# A worked example: the section {#example}

The Rules follow one medieval retinue. This chapter follows a section of the 1st Newfoundland
Regiment at [Beaumont-Hamel](#field-beaumont-hamel-1916) on 1 July 1916, from St John's Road to the
Danger Tree. It uses the same rules and the [Modern fire](rules.html#fire) module, and, where
dice are rolled, the dice given. The [team builder](team.html) made the section.

!!! example "The section"
    The team builder rolls {{wx.team}} of the Newfoundland Regiment. **{{wx.lead}}** leads
    it: {{wx.lead_terms}} terms, each a year of the war, so he is {{wx.lead_age}}, with
    Command {{wx.lead_cmd}}. Its best shot is **{{wx.rif}}**, who fires his rifle at
    **{{wx.rif_att}}%**. Its bomber is **{{wx.bomb}}**, and its stretcher-bearer is
    **{{wx.sb}}**, Leechcraft {{wx.sb_skill}}. {{wx.followers}} riflemen follow them. Their
    kit: a {{wx.kit}}, and no body armour.

## On the field map {#example-field}

!!! example "The section: St John's Road"
    At 09:15 the section leaves St John's Road with the rest of A Company, in extended order:
    {{wx.x_ext}} men to a hex, {{wx.x_move}} hexes a move, and shots at them count half, as
    in [Modern infantry](rules.html#modern-infantry). The day is fair, and the machine guns of
    RIR 119 can see them from the moment they cross the crest. Each gun fires {{wx.x_mg}}
    rounds a minute, one in {{wx.x_aimed}} rolled as an aimed shot, and its cone falls on
    the next unit behind. Every serious or critical wound puts a man down, as in
    [Modern fire puts men down](rules.html#modern-down), and a light one calls for the stop
    check.

    The gaps in the British wire bunch the men together. Under the guns the section checks at
    the end of each exchange to see whether it is **pinned**, with +{{wx.x_prone}} if it has
    gone to ground. Pinned, it cannot move. Shells burst among them too: a man struck rolls a
    wound as one hit by shell fragments at {{wx.i_attack}}%, as in
    [Fire support](rules.html#support).

## In the roller {#example-roller}

The heroes' wounds are rolled in full, as in [the fight](rules.html#fight).

!!! example "The section: the machine gun"
    A German machine-gunner, a veteran, fires at {{wx.rif_first}} at {{wx.g_attack}}% and
    rolls **{{wx.g_roll}}**: a margin of {{wx.g_margin}}. Against a bullet, a leg takes a
    margin of {{wx.g_band}} or more to be serious, so this one is **{{wx.g_sev}}**.
    {{wx.rif_first}} dodges at {{wx.g_def}}% and rolls {{wx.g_droll}}: no.

    The round strikes his **{{wx.g_loc}}**. {{wx.g_fracture}}It bleeds {{wx.g_bleed}}, pain
    {{wx.g_pain}}, and the leg is hurt. The wound carries shock, so he makes a stop check:
    Nerve {{wx.g_nerve}}, less {{wx.g_pain}} steps of pain, gives **{{wx.g_target}}**. He
    rolls {{wx.g_sroll}} and {{wx.g_result}}. At {{wx.g_per}} Blood a round, his
    {{wx.g_blood}} Blood lasts {{wx.g_rounds}} rounds.

!!! example "The section: the stretcher-bearer"
    {{wx.sb_first}} went over with the section, and the GM rules that he is close enough to
    reach {{wx.rif_first}} in time. He binds the thigh at Leechcraft {{wx.m_skill}} and rolls
    {{wx.m_roll}}: he binds it, taking the bleeding down {{wx.m_steps}} steps to
    **{{wx.m_after}}**, as in [First aid](rules.html#first-aid). He cannot carry him back
    under the guns. Like most of the Newfoundland wounded, {{wx.rif_first}} lies out in the
    grass until dark.

!!! example "The section: the helmet"
    Near the Danger Tree a shell bursts over {{wx.bomb_first}}. A shell fragment strikes at
    {{wx.s_attack}}%, with no dodge, and the dice give **{{wx.s_roll}}**: a margin of
    {{wx.s_margin}}. Against a fragment the head is serious from {{wx.s_band}} and critical
    from {{wx.s_crit}}, so this is **{{wx.s_sev}}**.

    It strikes his **{{wx.s_loc}}**, under his {{wx.s_armour}}. The helmet takes
    {{wx.s_steps_text}} off a fragment, so the wound is **{{wx.s_after}}**.
    {{wx.s_fracture}}It bleeds {{wx.s_bleed}}, pain {{wx.s_pain}}, and he is {{wx.s_impair}}.
    The stop check: Nerve {{wx.s_nerve}}, less {{wx.s_pain}} steps of pain, gives
    **{{wx.s_target}}**. He rolls {{wx.s_sroll}} and {{wx.s_result}}. Against a bullet the
    same helmet takes off {{wx.s_rifle}}: it was made for shrapnel.

    After dark the stretcher-bearers bring in the men they can find. The wounded go back by
    motor ambulance, as in [After the battle](rules.html#after), and onto the track, as in
    [Recovery](rules.html#recovery).

!!! gm "For the GM"
    The Newfoundlanders were on the field map for about half an hour, and the field map is
    where they were destroyed: of about 780 who went forward, 68 answered the roll the next
    morning. Run the exchange for the regiment and the roller for the section, and let the
    players see how little a good shot or a good stretcher-bearer can do against a machine
    gun in the open.
