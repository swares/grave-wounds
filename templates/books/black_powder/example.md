# A worked example: the company {#example}

The Rules follow one medieval retinue. This chapter follows a company of British foot at
[Albuera](#field-albuera-1811) on 16 May 1811, in Colborne's brigade, through the morning the Polish
lancers came out of the hail. It uses the same rules and the
[Black-powder guns](rules.html#mod-black-powder) module, and, where dice are rolled, the dice
given. The [team builder](team.html) made the company.

!!! example "The company"
    The team builder rolls {{bx.team}} of the Peninsular army. **{{bx.lead}}** leads it:
    {{bx.lead_terms}} terms, so he is {{bx.lead_age}}, with Command {{bx.lead_cmd}}. Its
    sergeant is **{{bx.sgt}}**. Its best shot is **{{bx.line}}**, who fires his musket at
    **{{bx.line_att}}%** but defends at only {{bx.line_def}}%, with Bayonet
    {{bx.line_bayonet}}. Its {{bx.sur_career}} is **{{bx.sur}}**, Leechcraft
    {{bx.sur_skill}}. {{bx.followers}} men of the line follow them. They wear no armour.

## On the field map {#example-field}

!!! example "The company: out of the hail"
    The company stands in Colborne's brigade, in line, firing into the flank of Girard's
    column. The field opens overcast. When the lancers come, the field's own phase makes the
    weather a storm (six exchanges after the brigade first fires: no source gives the time), as
    the accounts tell it: a squall of rain and hail from the north. No one can see to aim
    past {{bx.storm_sight}}, and each flintlock that fires misfires {{bx.mf_storm}}% of the
    time ({{bx.mf_rain}}% in plain rain), as in [Misfires](rules.html#misfire).

    The Polish lancers and hussars come out of the hail round the brigade's open flank, and
    are on it before it can turn. A lance strikes from {{bx.lance_reach}} hexes away, a
    bayonet from {{bx.bayonet_reach}}, so a man on foot must close with the horseman to reach
    him. When the battalions break, the rout and the pursuit run as in
    [Morale and the rout](rules.html#morale).

## In the roller {#example-roller}

The heroes' wounds are rolled in full, as in [the fight](rules.html#fight).

!!! example "The company: the ball"
    Before the storm, a French voltigeur, a regular, fires at {{bx.line_first}} at
    {{bx.b_attack}}% and rolls **{{bx.b_roll}}**: a margin of {{bx.b_margin}}. Against a ball,
    a leg takes a margin of {{bx.b_band}} or more to be serious, so this one is
    **{{bx.b_sev}}**. {{bx.line_first}} dodges at {{bx.b_def}}% and rolls {{bx.b_droll}}: no.

    The ball strikes his **{{bx.b_loc}}**. {{bx.b_fracture}}It bleeds {{bx.b_bleed}}, pain
    {{bx.b_pain}}, and the leg is hurt. The wound carries shock, so he makes a stop check:
    Nerve {{bx.b_nerve}}, less {{bx.b_pain}} steps of pain, gives **{{bx.b_target}}**. He
    rolls {{bx.b_sroll}} and {{bx.b_result}}. At {{bx.b_per}} Blood a round, his
    {{bx.b_blood}} Blood lasts {{bx.b_rounds}} rounds.

!!! example "The company: the surgeon's mate"
    The best Leechcraft in the company is {{bx.sur_first}}'s, and the GM rules that he can
    reach {{bx.line_first}} from behind the line in time. He binds the thigh at Leechcraft
    {{bx.m_skill}} and rolls {{bx.m_roll}}: he binds it, taking the bleeding down
    {{bx.m_steps}} steps to **{{bx.m_after}}**, as in [First aid](rules.html#first-aid).
    {{bx.line_first}}'s broken thigh keeps him out of the fight.

!!! example "The company: the lance"
    Then the lancers. One rides at {{bx.sgt_first}} from behind as the line gives way. The
    lancer is a veteran: he thrusts at {{bx.l_attack}}% and rolls **{{bx.l_roll}}**, a margin of
    {{bx.l_margin}}. A lance uses the ordinary bands, serious from {{bx.l_band}}, so this is
    **{{bx.l_sev}}**. {{bx.sgt_first}} turns to parry at {{bx.l_def}}% and rolls
    {{bx.l_droll}}: no.

    The point goes into his **{{bx.l_loc}}**. It bleeds {{bx.l_bleed}}, pain {{bx.l_pain}},
    and his {{bx.l_impair}} is hurt. It carries no shock, so there is no stop check, and he
    keeps his feet. But a musket takes both hands, and with an arm hurt he cannot fire it, as
    in [Wounds](rules.html#penalties), so he can only defend. Untreated, the wound
    {{bx.l_lethal}}: it is for the surgeon tonight, and then the camp, as in
    [Recovery](rules.html#recovery).

!!! gm "For the GM"
    At Albuera the company is a score of men in a brigade, and the field map decides what
    happens to the brigade: Oman says three of Colborne's four battalions were nearly destroyed
    within five minutes. Keep the roller for the heroes inside it: a ball in the thigh, a
    surgeon's mate who gets there in time, a sergeant who stays on his feet with a lance
    wound in his back.
