# Money by era {#money}

A **Day** is a common soldier's day's pay in his army's coin: the rules count in Days so that a
price in 1415 and one in 2008 can be weighed against each other, but every price was set down in
the money of its time, and the pages show that money first, with the Days after it. Where a coin
has no soldier's pay to measure it by, its prices stand in the coin alone.
