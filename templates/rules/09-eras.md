# Other wars {#eras}

Everything so far has used the medieval tables. The same rules carry a team through
{{e_others}} more eras, from the Thirty Years' War to Afghanistan. Each era has its own
skills, origins, careers, events, armies, teams and followers. Pick one with **Era** in the
[team builder](team.html).

## The eras {#era-list}

{{table:eras}}

Brawl, Parry and Dodge carry over to every era, and so do the skills used outside a fight
(Leechcraft, Fieldcraft, Watch, Stealth and the rest). Bow, Crossbow, Axe and Sling stay
medieval (the Plains nations have their own Bow). Gunnery is the skill of men who serve guns,
mortars and, in Korea, rocket launchers and recoilless rifles. From Vietnam on, the RPG is fired with Grenade and rocket.

## What changes {#era-changes}

- **A term.** Before 1914 a career term is {{e_term}} years, as in the medieval tables.
  From the First World War on, a term is {{e_ww_term}} year of the war, so a soldier of
  three terms is about twenty-one.
- **Wound tables.** Each army rolls on its own war's wound table, both for old wounds when
  a character is made and in the roller. Where a war's records have no wounds of some
  kind, the table borrows the nearest one: the pike is rolled as a long spear, and the
  frontier's gunshots come from the Civil War table.
- **Armour.** It follows the army and the year:
  - pikemen's and cuirassiers' armours in the 1630s;
  - the steel helmet from 1915–16;
  - nylon flak vests in Korea and Vietnam;
  - plate armour in Iraq and Afghanistan.

  Many armies wore none. A vest's rating against each threat (fragments, pistol rounds,
  rifle rounds) decides how many steps it takes off a wound, so a vest that stops a
  grenade fragment may do nothing against a rifle bullet. The roller and the field map read
  the same kits.
- **Kit and weapons** come with the career and the army: a Viet Cong RPG gunner carries an
  RPG, a Second World War tank crewman a submachine gun.
- **Names** are common names of the period and place, put together at random, and are not
  meant to name anyone. Chinese, Korean and Vietnamese names are written family name first.

## Women under arms {#women}

Where the records show women serving in a career, the team builder can roll a woman for it
at that army's share. In every other army a career rolls a man, and the page gives the
reason, since nurses and auxiliaries served but not in these careers. Each share is the
women among the army's soldiers as a whole, not just in that career, so treat it as a rough
guide. A GM may prefer to choose.

!!! gm "For the GM"
    Pick an era for the fighting you want, not the uniforms. Before the machine gun, battles
    are decided in the press and the rout, as in the medieval chapters. From 1914 on, men
    are put down by fire they never see, and what matters is cover, going to ground and
    the guns. The next chapters cover that.
