# Modules {#modules}

The core rules are written for blades, bows and armour, and they hold for every period. A
**module** adds what a period's weapons bring onto the field. Each period book says which modules
it switches on; leave the others out.

| Module | What it brings | Switched on by |
|---|---|---|
| [Black-powder guns](#mod-black-powder) | Batteries, canister and round shot; misfires in the wet; storming works with the bayonet | [The Black-Powder Wars](book-black-powder.html) |
| [Modern fire](#fire) | Extended order, rifles, machine guns, going to ground, digging in; fire support and barrages; trenches and bunkers; fixed lines; more men put down | [The World Wars](book-world-wars.html), [Korea and Vietnam](book-korea-vietnam.html) and the late wars; [The Black-Powder Wars](book-black-powder.html) for its breech-loaders only |
| [Armour and mines](#vehicles) | Tanks, carriers and lorries; anti-tank guns; minefields and sappers; riders | [The World Wars](book-world-wars.html), [Korea and Vietnam](book-korea-vietnam.html) and the late wars |
| [Air and night devices](#air) | Night vision; aircraft and air controllers; landing by air | [The World Wars](book-world-wars.html) for aircraft from 1944; [Korea and Vietnam](book-korea-vietnam.html) and the late wars |
