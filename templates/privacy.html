Privacy

Grave Wounds is a free game site run by Scott Wares. This page says what it collects, which is very little.

Counting visits

The start page, How to play and the books use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits. It uses no cookies and does not track you from site to site. I see totals, such as visits and pages viewed, and not who you are. Cloudflare handles that data under its own privacy policy.

Your game

Your teams, campaigns and saved games stay in your own browser. They are not sent to me.

If you play online, a small server passes the connection codes between your pages and forgets them after 6 hours. Your game then runs directly between the players' browsers.

The narrator is off until you turn it on. If you do, the page sends the battle's events to the AI service you chose, using your own key, and that service's terms apply.

Email updates

If you sign up for updates, your email address is held by Kit, the service that sends them. You confirm by email before you are added, and every email has an unsubscribe link. I use the address only for news about the game, and I do not sell or share it.

Telling me what you think

The feedback form is a Google Form, and your answers are stored by Google. Your email address is optional there, and I use it only to reply. Please don't put anything private in your comment. Google handles that data under its own privacy policy.
