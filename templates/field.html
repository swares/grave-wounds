Grave Wounds Field Map

Battles of hundreds: the rank and file fight as units, a minute an exchange, each hex 10 m. Set up the units, then each exchange side A moves, side B moves, and every fight and volley is resolved together through the wound tables. Morale decides most fights; the rout does most of the killing.

The field

Each unit is a block of hexes along its front (the dark edge), facing the arrow. Close order holds 40 men a hex, open order 20. Units touching across a front fight; archers shoot at the nearest enemy in range in front of them.
